    The realm

    Choose your race and set out into lands that grow more dangerous the further you travel. Every zone has its level, its monsters and its rewards; some let heroes fight each other.

    • There are no classes. You decide where every point goes, and your stats have no cap.
    • Win gold, gems and relic gear in battle, and master the weapons and elements you fight with.
    • Take on quests, join world events and boss hunts, and talk with the realm in chat.
